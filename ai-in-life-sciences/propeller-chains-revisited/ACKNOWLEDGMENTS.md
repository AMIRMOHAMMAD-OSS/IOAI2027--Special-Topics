# Credit and provenance

The scientific starting point is Ariel B. Lindner, Flavio Grynszpan, and Silvio E. Biali's 1993 paper, *Transmission of Internal Rotations: Correlated, Uncorrelated, and Localized Disrotatory Rotations in Propeller Chains*, [doi:10.1021/jo00076a027](https://doi.org/10.1021/jo00076a027).

This independent project is maintained by AmirMohammad MohammadHosseini. The implementation, numerical exploration, checks, figures, and documentation were prepared with assistance from OpenAI ChatGPT. The repository preserves numerical evidence and explicit limitations so that the calculations can be inspected and repeated. The historical paper's authors are credited for their original scientific work; they are not listed as software contributors or implied to endorse this implementation.

RDKit supplies the MMFF94 model and energy derivatives. NumPy and SciPy supply numerical linear algebra and optimization. Matplotlib and Pillow generate the original figures and molecular rendering; FFmpeg encodes the animation. Refer to each dependency for its license.

The project contains an independently reconstructed explicit-atom molecular model and new calculation records. It does not redistribute the publisher's article or its figures. The new figures are generated from the project's own coordinates and numerical data.
