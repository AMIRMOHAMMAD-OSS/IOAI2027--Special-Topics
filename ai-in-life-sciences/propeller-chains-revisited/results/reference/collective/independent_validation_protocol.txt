INDEPENDENT VALIDATION OF EXACTLY CONSTRAINED COLLECTIVE CANDIDATES

Scope
-----
The molecular model is the existing explicit-atom, 99-atom MMFF94 system.
The old six torsion definitions and the two fixed winding patterns are retained.
No sequential calculation is repeated by this audit. Each constrained branch
is a local conditional candidate, not a global collective lower bound.

Equality problem and units
--------------------------
At a fixed progress t, minimize the bare MMFF94 E(x), subject to

    c_i(x,t) = wrap(phi_i(x) - a_i - t*delta_i) = 0, i = 1,...,6.

Coordinates are angstrom, angles radians, energy kcal/mol. The local wrapped
residual is smooth near zero. Singular bond axes/reference planes are rejected.
Multipliers are defined by L = E + lambda^T c; they have units kcal/mol/radian.
No torsion-penalty energy enters either the objective or the reported energy.

First-order checks
------------------
For Q spanning the 291-dimensional complement of the six rigid motions, set
J = dc/dx and A = JQ. Verify rank(A)=6. Obtain lambda independently by solving
A^T lambda = -Q^T grad(E) in least squares. Both the full Cartesian Lagrangian
gradient grad(E)+J^T lambda and the true constraint-tangent gradient must be
small. A nonzero bare molecular gradient is generally required to balance the
normal forces enforcing the constraints; it does not imply failed constrained
optimization.

The audit computes torsions and their analytical Jacobian using a separate
vector-calculus expression, compares against wrapped central differences in
every Cartesian coordinate, and compares against the solver Jacobian. It also
checks rotational/translational invariance. Solver status and its tolerance
parameter do not replace these measured residuals.

Second-order checks
-------------------
Let Z = Q*null(JQ). For this molecule Z has 285 orthonormal columns. Classify

    Z^T [ Hess(E) + sum_i lambda_i Hess(c_i) ] Z.

This is the Hessian of the Lagrangian restricted to the feasible tangent, not
the bare energy Hessian and not the matrix left after arbitrarily deleting
twelve eigenvalues. A resolved positive lowest eigenvalue supports a strict
local constrained minimum under the stated constraint qualification. A negative
tangent eigenvalue or unresolved near-zero mode cannot be labeled a verified
local constrained minimum.

The independent audit varies BOTH the energy-gradient and constraint-Jacobian
finite-difference steps, using 1e-4 and 5e-5 angstrom. It checks numerical symmetry
before symmetrization. As a separate check, it displaces along tangent directions,
locally retracts to the six exact equalities, and evaluates the energy-only
second difference. This validates the multiplier sign and the constraint
curvature contribution without reusing an energy-gradient derivative.

Continuation and profile maxima
-------------------------------
Keep forward, reverse, and independent starting branches separate. At a common
t, different local constrained minima may coexist. Taking the lowest energy
from whichever branch happens to be available constructs an envelope, not an
automatically realizable molecular trajectory. A branch switch needs an actual
fixed-identity connector and its complete energy/synchronization audit.

For one smooth, converged branch, the envelope theorem gives

    dE_min/dt = -lambda^T delta.

This can be compared with same-branch finite differences and used to refine
candidate profile maxima. At such a maximum, lambda^T delta may vanish while
lambda itself remains nonzero. The peak then is not an unconstrained stationary
point, and it must not be called an elementary transition state.

SLSQP accepted iterates generally need not satisfy the equalities and can have
large coordinate jumps. Retaining them is useful for diagnosis; they cannot
be used directly as an exactly synchronized physical path.

Physical path checks
--------------------
Use one fixed atom identity throughout each supplied path. Do not align,
permute, reflect, or switch branches independently at each frame. The proper
graph-equivalent B representation is determined once for endpoint identification;
any final connector to exact B is recorded and checked. Opposite enantiomers
are not merged by proper rotations/chemical permutations.

Measure every original coordinate gap. A finely subdivided large Cartesian
chord is still that same physical chord; subdivision alone does not repair its
geometry, enforce constraints, or remove an energy overshoot. Record maximum
atomic displacement and examine energies and actual torsion progress throughout
any connector. For the explicit piecewise-Cartesian path, the independent audit
checks all stored frames and each quarter/midpoint/three-quarter gap probe.

Exact constraints at optimized grid points do not imply exact constraints
between them. The audit reports stored-frame and bridge-probe angular errors
separately (default limits 1e-8 and 1e-4 radians respectively), along with all
six progress fractions and a 0.02-angstrom maximum atomic gap. If a stricter
feasible path is required, construct and record small projected continuation
segments; do not silently call an approximate chord exactly feasible.

These finite checks are numerical evidence, not a mathematical continuous
supremum certificate. Report peak stability under a finer same-branch progress
mesh and stricter solver tolerance. Report unresolved folds, branch gaps,
failed starts, and distinct basins rather than silently dropping them.

Evidence interpretation
-----------------------
Fully checked branches establish explicit locally relaxed conditional paths
for the specified six-coordinate schedules. They improve on the previous
iteration-limited penalty trajectories. They do not prove that all collective
motions have been searched, that every collective route has a larger barrier,
or that the sequential route is globally optimal. A globally minimized energy
at each independent t would itself be only a slice envelope until continuity
between its minimizing basins was established. No free energies, rates, or
kinetic branching probabilities are inferred from these optimization paths.
