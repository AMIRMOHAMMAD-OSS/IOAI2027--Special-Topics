Local motion is still correlated motion

At five of the six saddles, an adjacent ring pair dominates the torsion change. The localized sequence connects opposite enantiomeric minima through several intermediate conformations. Playback shows geometry, not a simulated time scale.

A list of minima is not yet a path

The synchronized candidates constrain six torsions to a common progress parameter. Their central branch changes are joined by checked fixed-progress saddle connections. The original discrete node sequences are also available, with playback disabled across those unvalidated jumps.

Source: A. B. Lindner, F. Grynszpan & S. E. Biali, J. Org. Chem. 58, 6662–6670 (1993), doi:10.1021/jo00076a027.
